### Comparable games
Research includes asymmetric/cooperative games such as It Takes Two, Split Fiction, We Were Here, The Past Within, BOKURA, Tick Tock: A Tale for Two, Operation: Tango, Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes, CouchEscape and The Click.

The purpose of comparable-game research is not to copy mechanics, but to distinguish established asymmetric-information patterns from this project's intended focus on **asymmetric agency + causal merge + emergent consequences**.

## Research discipline

For every material architecture conclusion, separate:

- **FACT** — directly supported by source material.
- **INFERENCE** — conclusion drawn from one or more facts.
- **PROPOSAL** — suggested project design.
- **DECISION** — only after explicit acceptance and ADR recording.

Add concrete citations and source links here as research is performed; do not populate this file with unverified secondary summaries.
